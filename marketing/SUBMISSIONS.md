## Description (~120 words)
USFreeTools is a collection of 155 free online tools that run entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded or stored. It covers text tools (word and character counters, case converter, duplicate remover), developer utilities (JSON/XML/SQL formatters, Base64, JWT decoder, regex tester), security (password generator, hash generator), and more than 60 calculators for home and garden (paver, concrete, mulch, tile, drywall), money (mortgage, loan, tip, sales tax), fitness (BMI, calorie deficit, pace) and everyday maths. Alongside the tools are 7,700+ ready-made answer pages — "170 cm to inches", "15% of 200" — each with the exact result, formula and worked steps. No account, no limits, no downloads.
