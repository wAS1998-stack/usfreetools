Health and Fitness Calculators

Tools such as the BMI, calorie, body fat and pregnancy due date calculators apply general population formulas. They cannot account for your body composition, medical history, medication or pregnancy, and they are not a diagnosis, a screening test, or a substitute for advice from a doctor or registered dietitian. BMI in particular is a crude population-level measure and is a poor guide for athletes, older adults, pregnant people and children. Talk to a qualified clinician before acting on any result.

Financial Calculators

Tools such as the mortgage, loan, compound interest, down payment and sales tax calculators produce arithmetic estimates from the figures you supply. They do not include fees, insurance, taxes, closing costs, rate changes, overpayments or local rules unless you enter them, so a real quote from a lender, broker or tax authority will usually differ. Nothing on this site is financial, mortgage, investment or tax advice, and we are not a licensed financial adviser, broker or tax agent.

Construction and Material Calculators

Material estimates for concrete, paint, mulch, gravel, decking, drywall and similar jobs assume simple shapes and typical coverage rates, and the prices shown are editable placeholders based on broad US averages rather than quotes. Waste, offcuts, site conditions, delivery minimums and local pricing all vary. Order a sensible margin above any figure here and confirm quantities with your supplier or contractor before buying.
