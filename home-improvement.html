Getting quantities right

Most DIY overspending, and most mid-project trips to the store, come from three mistakes. One is measuring the visible surface and forgetting the layer underneath: gravel under pavers, compacted base under concrete, topsoil under sod. Another is skipping the waste allowance. Every edge needs a cut, and offcuts rarely get reused, so add 10% for straight layouts and 15% for curves and diagonals. The third is buying in the wrong unit: bulk materials are sold by the cubic yard, bagged ones by the cubic foot, so check which you are pricing before comparing.

These calculators handle all three. Enter your dimensions in feet and inches and they return the quantity to order in the unit the store sells, with waste included. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is stored.
