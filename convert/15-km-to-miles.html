15 km to miles

15 km = 9.3206 mi

Formula: miles = km × 0.621371. Rounded to 2 decimals: 9.32 mi. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 15 km to mi

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 mi = 1.609344 km
  2. Divide: 15 ÷ 1.609344
  3. Result: 9.3206 mi

So 15 kilometers is 9.3206 miles. For most everyday purposes 9.3 mi is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 15 km means in practice

Distances of 15 km are the everyday kind: commutes, runs, walks and cycling loops. Fitness apps, treadmills and car odometers switch between km and miles depending on region, so 15 km = 9.32 mi is worth memorising in both directions. A quick estimate: multiply km by 0.6 to get miles, or miles by 1.6 to get km.
