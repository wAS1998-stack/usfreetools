3.1 miles to km

3.1 mi = 4.989 km

Formula: km = miles × 1.609344. Rounded to 2 decimals: 4.99 km. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 3.1 mi to km

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 mi = 1.609344 km
  2. Multiply: 3.1 × 1.609344
  3. Result: 4.989 km

So 3.1 miles is 4.989 kilometers. For most everyday purposes 5 km is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 3.1 mi means in practice

3.1 mi is the distance of a 5K, so this conversion is mostly looked up by runners comparing race distances, training plans and pace charts across metric and imperial. 4.989 km is 3.1 miles; at a 10 min/mile pace that is roughly 31 minutes, and at 5 min/km about 25 minutes.
