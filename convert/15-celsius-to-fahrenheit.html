15 Celsius to Fahrenheit

15 °C = 59 °F (288.15 K)

Formula: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. Rounded to 2 decimals: 59 °F. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 15 °C to °F

  1. Multiply 15 by 9: 135
  2. Divide by 5: 27
  3. Add 32: 59 °F

So 15 Celsius is 59 Fahrenheit. For most everyday purposes 59 °F is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 15 °C means in practice

15 °C is cool, typical of spring and autumn days or a chilly room. In Fahrenheit that is 59 °F, in Celsius 15 °C. The two scales meet at −40°, and a handy estimate is to double the Celsius value and add 30 (60 °F here, versus the exact 59 °F).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Fahrenheit is 15 °C?

15 °C equals 59 °F. The conversion uses the formula °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, which is exact — the only rounding is in how many decimals you keep.

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. For 15 °C: Multiply 15 by 9: 135 → Divide by 5: 27 → Add 32: 59 °F.

How do I convert 59 °F back to °C?

Reverse the formula — see the fahrenheit to celsius converter. 59 °F is 15 °C (small differences come from rounding).

Is 15 °C to °F the same in the US and UK?

Yes for this unit pair — inches, pounds, miles and °F are defined identically in both countries. The exception is gallons: US and imperial gallons differ by about 20%.
