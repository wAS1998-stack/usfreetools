20 inches to cm

20 in = 50.8 cm

Formula: cm = inches × 2.54. Rounded to 2 decimals: 50.8 cm. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 20 in to cm

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 in = 2.54 cm
  2. Multiply: 20 × 2.54
  3. Result: 50.8 cm

So 20 inches is 50.8 centimeters. For most everyday purposes 50.8 cm is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 20 in means in practice

At this size the conversion usually comes up for products and parts: screen diagonals, paper and frame sizes, tyre widths, furniture dimensions and sewing patterns. A 20 in measurement is 50.8 cm, or 20 inches — for a quick mental check, remember that 10 cm is just under 4 inches and 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm.
