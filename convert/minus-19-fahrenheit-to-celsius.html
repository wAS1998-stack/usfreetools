-19 Fahrenheit to Celsius

-19 °F = -28.3333 °C (244.82 K)

Formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Rounded to 2 decimals: -28.33 °C. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert -19 °F to °C

  1. Subtract 32 from -19: -51
  2. Multiply by 5: -255
  3. Divide by 9: -28.3333 °C

So -19 Fahrenheit is -28.3333 Celsius. For most everyday purposes -28.3 °C is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What -19 °F means in practice

-19 °F is bitterly cold — polar or deep-winter weather where exposed skin is at risk and water freezes quickly. In Fahrenheit that is -19 °F, in Celsius -28.3 °C. The two scales meet at −40°, and a handy estimate is to double the Celsius value and add 30 (-26 °F here, versus the exact -19 °F).
