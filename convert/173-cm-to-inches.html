173 cm to inches

173 cm = 68.1102 in (5 ft 8.11 in)

Formula: inches = cm ÷ 2.54. Rounded to 2 decimals: 68.11 in. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 173 cm to in

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 in = 2.54 cm
  2. Divide: 173 ÷ 2.54
  3. Result: 68.1102 in

So 173 centimeters is 68.1102 inches. For most everyday purposes 68.1 in is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 173 cm means in practice

173 cm is in the range of adult and teenage heights, which is the most common reason people look this conversion up. Medical forms, passports, dating profiles and sports rosters in the United States use feet and inches, while most of the world uses centimeters. 173 cm works out to 5 ft 8.11 in; on a form that only accepts whole inches you would write 68 in.
