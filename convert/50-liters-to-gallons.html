50 liters to gallons

50 L = 13.2086 gal (10.998 UK gal)

Formula: gallons = liters ÷ 3.78541. Rounded to 2 decimals: 13.21 gal. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 50 L to gal

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 gal = 3.785412 L
  2. Divide: 50 ÷ 3.785412
  3. Result: 13.2086 gal

So 50 liters is 13.2086 US gallons. For most everyday purposes 13.2 gal is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 50 L means in practice

Volumes of 50 L usually mean fuel tanks, water heaters, pools or rain barrels. 50 litres equals 13.21 US gallons (11 UK gallons). Fuel is sold per litre in most countries and per gallon in the US, so this figure also converts a tank size: 13.21 gal at $3.50/gal costs about $46.23.

Is 50 L to gal the same in the US and UK?

No. This page uses US gallons (3.785 L). An imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 L, so the UK figure is about 17% smaller — it is shown in brackets above.
