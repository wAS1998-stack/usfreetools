7 feet to meters

7 ft = 2.1336 m

Formula: meters = feet × 0.3048. Rounded to 2 decimals: 2.13 m. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 7 ft to m

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 m = 3.28084 ft
  2. Divide: 7 ÷ 3.28084
  3. Result: 2.1336 m

So 7 feet is 2.1336 meters. For most everyday purposes 2.1 m is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 7 ft means in practice

7 ft is in the range of adult and teenage heights, which is the most common reason people look this conversion up. Medical forms, passports, dating profiles and sports rosters in the United States use feet and inches, while most of the world uses centimeters. 213.36 cm works out to 7 ft 0 in; on a form that only accepts whole inches you would write 84 in.
