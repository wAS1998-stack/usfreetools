33 feet to meters

33 ft = 10.0584 m

Formula: meters = feet × 0.3048. Rounded to 2 decimals: 10.06 m. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 33 ft to m

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 m = 3.28084 ft
  2. Divide: 33 ÷ 3.28084
  3. Result: 10.0584 m

So 33 feet is 10.0584 meters. For most everyday purposes 10.1 m is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 33 ft means in practice

Lengths this large tend to be room dimensions, fabric, fencing, vehicle sizes or sporting distances. 33 ft is 10.06 metres, 396 inches or 33 feet. For building work, round to the nearest ¼ inch (396 in) and keep the metric figure for the order sheet.
