4 cm to inches

4 cm = 1.5748 in

Formula: inches = cm ÷ 2.54. Rounded to 2 decimals: 1.57 in. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 4 cm to in

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 in = 2.54 cm
  2. Divide: 4 ÷ 2.54
  3. Result: 1.5748 in

So 4 centimeters is 1.5748 inches. For most everyday purposes 1.6 in is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 4 cm means in practice

At this size the conversion usually comes up for products and parts: screen diagonals, paper and frame sizes, tyre widths, furniture dimensions and sewing patterns. A 4 cm measurement is 4 cm, or 1.57 inches — for a quick mental check, remember that 10 cm is just under 4 inches and 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm.
