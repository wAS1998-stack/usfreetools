18 Fahrenheit to Celsius

18 °F = -7.7778 °C (265.37 K)

Formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Rounded to 2 decimals: -7.78 °C. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 18 °F to °C

  1. Subtract 32 from 18: -14
  2. Multiply by 5: -70
  3. Divide by 9: -7.7778 °C

So 18 Fahrenheit is -7.7778 Celsius. For most everyday purposes -7.8 °C is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 18 °F means in practice

18 °F is cold winter weather, around or below freezing — coat, hat and gloves territory. In Fahrenheit that is 18 °F, in Celsius -7.8 °C. The two scales meet at −40°, and a handy estimate is to double the Celsius value and add 30 (14 °F here, versus the exact 18 °F).

18 °F equals -7.7778 °C. The conversion uses the formula °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9, which is exact — the only rounding is in how many decimals you keep.

How do I convert -7.78 °C back to °F?

Is 18 °F to °C the same in the US and UK?
