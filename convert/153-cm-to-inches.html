153 cm to inches

153 cm = 60.2362 in (5 ft 0.24 in)

Formula: inches = cm ÷ 2.54. Rounded to 2 decimals: 60.24 in. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 153 cm to in

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 in = 2.54 cm
  2. Divide: 153 ÷ 2.54
  3. Result: 60.2362 in

So 153 centimeters is 60.2362 inches. For most everyday purposes 60.2 in is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 153 cm means in practice

153 cm is in the range of adult and teenage heights, which is the most common reason people look this conversion up. Medical forms, passports, dating profiles and sports rosters in the United States use feet and inches, while most of the world uses centimeters. 153 cm works out to 5 ft 0.24 in; on a form that only accepts whole inches you would write 60 in.
