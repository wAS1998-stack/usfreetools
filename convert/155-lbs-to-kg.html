155 lbs to kg

155 lb = 70.3068 kg

Formula: kg = lb × 0.453592. Rounded to 2 decimals: 70.31 kg. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 155 lb to kg

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 kg = 2.204623 lb
  2. Divide: 155 ÷ 2.204623
  3. Result: 70.3068 kg

So 155 pounds is 70.3068 kilograms. For most everyday purposes 70.3 kg is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.
