-33 Celsius to Fahrenheit

-33 °C = -27.4 °F (240.15 K)

Formula: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. Rounded to 2 decimals: -27.4 °F. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert -33 °C to °F

  1. Multiply -33 by 9: -297
  2. Divide by 5: -59.4
  3. Add 32: -27.4 °F

So -33 Celsius is -27.4 Fahrenheit. For most everyday purposes -27.4 °F is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What -33 °C means in practice

-33 °C is bitterly cold — polar or deep-winter weather where exposed skin is at risk and water freezes quickly. In Fahrenheit that is -27.4 °F, in Celsius -33 °C. The two scales meet at −40°, and a handy estimate is to double the Celsius value and add 30 (-36 °F here, versus the exact -27.4 °F).
