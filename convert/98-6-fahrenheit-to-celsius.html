98.6 Fahrenheit to Celsius

98.6 °F = 37 °C (310.15 K)

Formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Rounded to 2 decimals: 37 °C. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 98.6 °F to °C

  1. Subtract 32 from 98.6: 66.6
  2. Multiply by 5: 333
  3. Divide by 9: 37 °C

So 98.6 Fahrenheit is 37 Celsius. For most everyday purposes 37 °C is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 98.6 °F means in practice

98.6 °F is hot — heat-wave or desert conditions where hydration matters. In Fahrenheit that is 98.6 °F, in Celsius 37 °C. A body temperature of 37 °C (98.6 °F) is normal. The two scales meet at −40°, and a handy estimate is to double the Celsius value and add 30 (104 °F here, versus the exact 98.6 °F).
