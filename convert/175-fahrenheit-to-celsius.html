175 Fahrenheit to Celsius

175 °F = 79.4444 °C (352.59 K)

Formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Rounded to 2 decimals: 79.44 °C. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 175 °F to °C

  1. Subtract 32 from 175: 143
  2. Multiply by 5: 715
  3. Divide by 9: 79.4444 °C

So 175 Fahrenheit is 79.4444 Celsius. For most everyday purposes 79.4 °C is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 175 °F means in practice

175 °F is far above outdoor temperatures: hot water, a hot bath (about 40 °C), simmering (85–95 °C) or boiling water (100 °C at sea level). In Fahrenheit that is 175 °F, in Celsius 79.4 °C. The two scales meet at −40°, and a handy estimate is to double the Celsius value and add 30 (188 °F here, versus the exact 175 °F).
