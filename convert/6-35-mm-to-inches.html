6.35 mm to inches

6.35 mm = 0.25 in

Formula: inches = mm ÷ 25.4. Rounded to 2 decimals: 0.25 in. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 6.35 mm to in

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 in = 25.4 mm
  2. Divide: 6.35 ÷ 25.4
  3. Result: 0.25 in

So 6.35 millimeters is 0.25 inches. For most everyday purposes 0.2 in is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 6.35 mm means in practice

At this size the conversion usually comes up for products and parts: screen diagonals, paper and frame sizes, tyre widths, furniture dimensions and sewing patterns. A 6.35 mm measurement is 0.64 cm, or 0.25 inches — for a quick mental check, remember that 10 cm is just under 4 inches and 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm.
