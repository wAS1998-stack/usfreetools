30 mph to km/h

30 mph = 48.2803 km/h

Formula: km/h = mph × 1.609344. Rounded to 2 decimals: 48.28 km/h. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 30 mph to km/h

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 mph = 1.609344 km/h
  2. Multiply: 30 × 1.609344
  3. Result: 48.2803 km/h

So 30 miles per hour is 48.2803 kilometers per hour. For most everyday purposes 48.3 km/h is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 30 mph means in practice

30 mph is typical urban and country-road driving speed. That is 48.3 km/h, 30 mph, or 13.41 m/s. Speed limits are posted in mph in the US and UK and in km/h almost everywhere else, so this conversion matters most to drivers hiring cars abroad; a quick estimate is mph ≈ km/h × 0.6. At this speed you cover 0.8 km (or 0.5 miles) every minute.
