1.5 kg to lbs

1.5 kg = 3.3069 lb

Formula: lb = kg × 2.20462. Rounded to 2 decimals: 3.31 lb. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 1.5 kg to lb

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 kg = 2.204623 lb
  2. Multiply: 1.5 × 2.204623
  3. Result: 3.3069 lb

So 1.5 kilograms is 3.3069 pounds. For most everyday purposes 3.3 lb is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 1.5 kg means in practice

Weights this small are typical of luggage, parcels, pets, babies and kitchen quantities. 1.5 kg is 1.5 kg or 3.31 lb — 52.91 ounces if you need the smaller unit. Airline cabin-bag limits are usually quoted in kg (7–10 kg) and checked bags in both (23 kg ≈ 50 lb), which is where most travellers meet this conversion.
