1 mm to inches

1 mm = 0.0394 in

Formula: inches = mm ÷ 25.4. Rounded to 2 decimals: 0.04 in. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 1 mm to in

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 in = 25.4 mm
  2. Divide: 1 ÷ 25.4
  3. Result: 0.0394 in

So 1 millimeters is 0.0394 inches. For most everyday purposes 0 in is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 1 mm means in practice

At this size the conversion usually comes up for products and parts: screen diagonals, paper and frame sizes, tyre widths, furniture dimensions and sewing patterns. A 1 mm measurement is 0.1 cm, or 0.04 inches — for a quick mental check, remember that 10 cm is just under 4 inches and 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many inches is 1 mm?

1 mm equals 0.0394 in. The conversion uses the formula inches = mm ÷ 25.4, which is exact — the only rounding is in how many decimals you keep.

What is the formula to convert millimeters to inches?

inches = mm ÷ 25.4. For 1 mm: Take the exact factor: 1 in = 25.4 mm → Divide: 1 ÷ 25.4 → Result: 0.0394 in.

How do I convert 0.04 in back to mm?

Reverse the formula — see the inches to mm converter. 0.04 in is 1 mm (small differences come from rounding).

Is 1 mm to in the same in the US and UK?

Yes for this unit pair — inches, pounds, miles and °F are defined identically in both countries. The exception is gallons: US and imperial gallons differ by about 20%.
