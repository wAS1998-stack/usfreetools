38 grams to oz

38 g = 1.3404 oz

Formula: oz = grams ÷ 28.3495. Rounded to 2 decimals: 1.34 oz. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 38 g to oz

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 oz = 28.349523 g
  2. Divide: 38 ÷ 28.349523
  3. Result: 1.3404 oz

So 38 grams is 1.3404 ounces. For most everyday purposes 1.3 oz is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 38 g means in practice

Weights this small are typical of luggage, parcels, pets, babies and kitchen quantities. 38 g is 0.04 kg or 0.08 lb — 1.34 ounces if you need the smaller unit. Airline cabin-bag limits are usually quoted in kg (7–10 kg) and checked bags in both (23 kg ≈ 50 lb), which is where most travellers meet this conversion.
