27 mph to km/h

27 mph = 43.4523 km/h

Formula: km/h = mph × 1.609344. Rounded to 2 decimals: 43.45 km/h. Change the number below to convert any other value.

How to convert 27 mph to km/h

  1. Take the exact factor: 1 mph = 1.609344 km/h
  2. Multiply: 27 × 1.609344
  3. Result: 43.4523 km/h

So 27 miles per hour is 43.4523 kilometers per hour. For most everyday purposes 43.5 km/h is precise enough; keep four decimals for engineering, medical or scientific work.

What 27 mph means in practice

27 mph is typical urban and country-road driving speed. That is 43.5 km/h, 27 mph, or 12.07 m/s. Speed limits are posted in mph in the US and UK and in km/h almost everywhere else, so this conversion matters most to drivers hiring cars abroad; a quick estimate is mph ≈ km/h × 0.6. At this speed you cover 0.72 km (or 0.45 miles) every minute.
