Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

USFreeTools.com ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require accounts or registration. The text you enter into our tools is processed entirely in your browser and is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

We may automatically collect non-personal information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location (country/region) through standard analytics.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We set no cookies of our own. The only cookies this site can place are Google Analytics measurement cookies, described below. We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; every tool on this site will continue to work normally if you do.

3. Advertising

This site does not display advertising. We do not run Google AdSense or any other ad network, we do not embed advertising or retargeting pixels, and we do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which tools people use and where the site is slow or broken. It records aggregated, non-identifying information such as page URL, referrer, approximate region, device and browser type. It does not receive anything you type into a tool. We also load web fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google's servers; no other third party receives a request from this site.

5. Data Security

Our site is served over HTTPS. Since tool data is processed locally in your browser, there is no transmission of your content to secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict use of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, reach us via our Contact Page.
