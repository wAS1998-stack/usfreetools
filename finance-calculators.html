How to use these together

  • Buying a home: start with the down payment you can afford, then put the remaining loan into the mortgage calculator to see the monthly principal and interest. Property tax, insurance and HOA fees come on top.
  • Comparing a job offer: convert the hourly rate to a yearly salary, add expected overtime, and compare the raise as a percentage of your current pay.
  • Saving for a goal: the compound interest calculator shows how monthly contributions grow; small changes in the rate or the number of years make a large difference.
  • These calculators give estimates for planning. Lenders, employers and tax authorities apply their own rules, so confirm the final figures with them before you commit.
