About USFreeTools

Free, fast tools that show their working. No signup, nothing uploaded.

What USFreeTools is

USFreeTools is an independent collection of 155 free browser tools: calculators, unit converters, text utilities and developer helpers. It also has more than 8,000 ready-worked answers, such as 170 cm to inches or 15% of 200. Nothing requires an account and nothing is paywalled.

Why it exists

Most quick-answer tools online are slow, cluttered, or ask you to sign up before showing a number. These are built the other way round: the answer appears as you type, the page explains how it was calculated, and the whole thing works on a phone.

What you will find

  • Home & Garden calculators: pavers, concrete, gravel, mulch, topsoil, sod, fences, decks, paint, drywall, tile and heating, with waste allowances built in.
  • Unit conversions and the height chart: exact figures with the formula shown.
  • Percentage tables: tips, tax, discounts and the reverse calculations.
  • Money, health and everyday maths: mortgage, loan, BMI, age and more.
  • Text and developer tools: word counter, JSON formatter, password generator and others.

How the numbers are checked

Every formula and constant is documented on the methodology page, along with the standards it comes from. Conversions use exact international definitions. Material estimates use common trade rules of thumb, and the page tells you when to confirm with a supplier or professional.

Privacy

All tools run in your browser. What you type is not uploaded, logged or stored. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits, as described in the privacy policy. There are currently no ads.

Contact

Found a wrong number, or want a tool that is missing? Use the contact page. Corrections are fixed quickly and noted on the methodology page.
