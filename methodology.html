Methodology

How every calculator and conversion on this site is worked out, and where the constants come from.

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Unit conversions

Length and mass conversions use the exact definitions set by the 1959 International Yard and Pound Agreement and published by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST):

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly, so 1 foot = 30.48 cm and 1 mile = 1.609344 km
  • 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly, so 1 ounce = 28.349523125 g
  • 1 US gallon = 231 cubic inches = 3.785411784 L exactly; 1 imperial (UK) gallon = 4.54609 L exactly
  • °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; K = °C + 273.15

Results are calculated at full precision and rounded only for display. Pages show four decimals where precision matters and two for everyday use.

Percentages

X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. "Y plus X%" multiplies by (1 + X/100), and "Y minus X%" by (1 − X/100). Reverse calculations divide rather than subtract, which is why removing a 10% discount is not the same as adding 10%.

Home and garden material estimates

  • Volume: area (sq ft) × depth (ft) ÷ 27 = cubic yards. Bulk materials are sold by the cubic yard and bagged ones by the cubic foot, so both are shown.
  • Waste: the default allowance is 10% for straight layouts. Use 15% for curves, diagonals and patterned layouts, where edge cuts multiply.
  • Base layers: paver and concrete guidance uses common residential practice, such as 4–6″ of compacted gravel under patios and 1″ of bedding sand. Driveways, frost-prone soil and structural work need local code and a professional's confirmation.
  • Coverage figures (paint per gallon, mulch per bag, sod per pallet) are typical manufacturer ranges. Check the label on the product you buy.

Money and health calculators

Loan and mortgage payments use the standard amortisation formula M = P·r(1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), with r the monthly rate. They exclude taxes, insurance and fees unless a field asks for them. BMI is weight (kg) ÷ height (m)² with the WHO adult categories. These tools are for estimates and planning, not financial or medical advice.

Corrections
