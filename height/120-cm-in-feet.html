120 cm in feet

120 cm = 3′11.24″

47.24 inches · 1.2 m · nearest: 3′11″. Change the value below for any other height.

How it is calculated

  1. Divide by 2.54: 120 ÷ 2.54 = 47.2441 inches
  2. Whole feet: 47.2441 ÷ 12 = 3 ft (remainder 11.2441 in)
  3. Result: 3 ft 11.24 in

This is a child's height, or a very short adult. Paediatric growth charts use centimetres, while US school and sports forms use feet and inches, which is why the conversion comes up for parents.
