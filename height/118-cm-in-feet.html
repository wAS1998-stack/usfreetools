118 cm in feet

118 cm = 3′10.46″

46.46 inches · 1.18 m · nearest: 3′10″. Change the value below for any other height.

How it is calculated

  1. Divide by 2.54: 118 ÷ 2.54 = 46.4567 inches
  2. Whole feet: 46.4567 ÷ 12 = 3 ft (remainder 10.4567 in)
  3. Result: 3 ft 10.46 in

This is a child's height, or a very short adult. Paediatric growth charts use centimetres, while US school and sports forms use feet and inches, which is why the conversion comes up for parents.
