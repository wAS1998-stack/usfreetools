133 cm in feet

133 cm = 4′4.36″

52.36 inches · 1.33 m · nearest: 4′4″. Change the value below for any other height.

How it is calculated

  1. Divide by 2.54: 133 ÷ 2.54 = 52.3622 inches
  2. Whole feet: 52.3622 ÷ 12 = 4 ft (remainder 4.3622 in)
  3. Result: 4 ft 4.36 in

This is a child's height, or a very short adult. Paediatric growth charts use centimetres, while US school and sports forms use feet and inches, which is why the conversion comes up for parents.
