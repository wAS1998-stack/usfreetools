139 cm in feet

139 cm = 4′6.72″

54.72 inches · 1.39 m · nearest: 4′7″. Change the value below for any other height.

How it is calculated

  1. Divide by 2.54: 139 ÷ 2.54 = 54.7244 inches
  2. Whole feet: 54.7244 ÷ 12 = 4 ft (remainder 6.7244 in)
  3. Result: 4 ft 6.72 in

This is a child's height, or a very short adult. Paediatric growth charts use centimetres, while US school and sports forms use feet and inches, which is why the conversion comes up for parents.
