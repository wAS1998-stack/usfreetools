What is 7.5% of 48?

7.5% of 48 = 3.6

48 × 7.5 ÷ 100 = 3.6. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 7.5% of 48

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 7.5% = 0.075
  2. Multiply: 48 × 0.075
  3. Result: 3.6

7.5% is a typical sales-tax or VAT rate. Tax on $48 at 7.5% comes to $3.6, so the price with tax is $51.6. If instead $48 already includes 7.5% tax, the pre-tax price was $44.65.
