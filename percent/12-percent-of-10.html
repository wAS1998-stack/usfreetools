What is 12% of 10?

12% of 10 = 1.2

10 × 12 ÷ 100 = 1.2. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 12% of 10

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 12% = 0.12
  2. Multiply: 10 × 0.12
  3. Result: 1.2

As a discount, 12% off $10 saves $1.2 and leaves a sale price of $8.8. As a raise or increase, 12% on 10 takes it up to 11.2. The 1.2 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 12% of 10?

12% of 10 is 1.2. Multiply 10 by 12 and divide by 100: 10 × 12 ÷ 100 = 1.2.

How do I calculate 12 percent of 10 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 10 is 1. Then scale: 12% is 1.2 times that, which gives 1.2.

What is 10 minus 12%?

10 − 12% = 8.8. Subtracting 12% is the same as multiplying by 0.88.

What is 10 plus 12%?

10 + 12% = 11.2. Adding 12% is the same as multiplying by 1.12.

1.2 is what percent of 10?

1.2 ÷ 10 × 100 = 12%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
