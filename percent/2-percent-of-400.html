What is 2% of 400?

2% of 400 = 8

400 × 2 ÷ 100 = 8. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 2% of 400

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 2% = 0.02
  2. Multiply: 400 × 0.02
  3. Result: 8

Small percentages like 2% usually show up as interest, fees, inflation or commission. 2% of 400 is 8 — on a 400 balance at 2% a year, that is the annual interest, or about 0.67 per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 2% of 400?

2% of 400 is 8. Multiply 400 by 2 and divide by 100: 400 × 2 ÷ 100 = 8.

How do I calculate 2 percent of 400 in my head?

Find 1% first: 1% of 400 is 4. Then multiply by 2: 8.

What is 400 minus 2%?

400 − 2% = 392. Subtracting 2% is the same as multiplying by 0.98.

What is 400 plus 2%?

400 + 2% = 408. Adding 2% is the same as multiplying by 1.02.

8 is what percent of 400?

8 ÷ 400 × 100 = 2%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
