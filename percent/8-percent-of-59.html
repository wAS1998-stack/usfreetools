What is 8% of 59?

8% of 59 = 4.72

59 × 8 ÷ 100 = 4.72. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 8% of 59

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 8% = 0.08
  2. Multiply: 59 × 0.08
  3. Result: 4.72
