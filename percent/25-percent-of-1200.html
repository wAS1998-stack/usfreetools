What is 25% of 1,200?

25% of 1,200 = 300

1,200 × 25 ÷ 100 = 300. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 25% of 1,200

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 25% = 0.25
  2. Multiply: 1,200 × 0.25
  3. Result: 300

As a discount, 25% off $1,200 saves $300 and leaves a sale price of $900. As a raise or increase, 25% on 1,200 takes it up to 1,500. The 300 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 25% of 1,200?

25% of 1,200 is 300. Multiply 1,200 by 25 and divide by 100: 1,200 × 25 ÷ 100 = 300.

How do I calculate 25 percent of 1,200 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 1,200 is 120. Then scale: 25% is 2.5 times that, which gives 300.

What is 1,200 minus 25%?

1,200 − 25% = 900. Subtracting 25% is the same as multiplying by 0.75.

What is 1,200 plus 25%?

1,200 + 25% = 1,500. Adding 25% is the same as multiplying by 1.25.

300 is what percent of 1,200?

300 ÷ 1,200 × 100 = 25%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
