What is 85% of 42?

85% of 42 = 35.7

42 × 85 ÷ 100 = 35.7. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 85% of 42

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 85% = 0.85
  2. Multiply: 42 × 0.85
  3. Result: 35.7

85% of 42 is 35.7. Put another way, 35.7 is 85% of 42, and 42 is 117.65% of 35.7 — percentages are just fractions of 100, so any of the three numbers can be found from the other two.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 85% of 42?

85% of 42 is 35.7. Multiply 42 by 85 and divide by 100: 42 × 85 ÷ 100 = 35.7.

How do I calculate 85 percent of 42 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 42 is 4.2. Then scale: 85% is 8.5 times that, which gives 35.7.

What is 42 minus 85%?

42 − 85% = 6.3. Subtracting 85% is the same as multiplying by 0.15.

What is 42 plus 85%?

42 + 85% = 77.7. Adding 85% is the same as multiplying by 1.85.

35.7 is what percent of 42?

35.7 ÷ 42 × 100 = 85%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
