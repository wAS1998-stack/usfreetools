What is 30% of 120?

30% of 120 = 36

120 × 30 ÷ 100 = 36. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 30% of 120

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 30% = 0.3
  2. Multiply: 120 × 0.3
  3. Result: 36

As a discount, 30% off $120 saves $36 and leaves a sale price of $84. As a raise or increase, 30% on 120 takes it up to 156. The 36 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 30% of 120?

30% of 120 is 36. Multiply 120 by 30 and divide by 100: 120 × 30 ÷ 100 = 36.

How do I calculate 30 percent of 120 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 120 is 12. Then scale: 30% is 3 times that, which gives 36.

What is 120 minus 30%?

120 − 30% = 84. Subtracting 30% is the same as multiplying by 0.7.

What is 120 plus 30%?

120 + 30% = 156. Adding 30% is the same as multiplying by 1.3.

36 is what percent of 120?

36 ÷ 120 × 100 = 30%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
