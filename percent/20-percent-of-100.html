What is 20% of 100?

20% of 100 = 20

100 × 20 ÷ 100 = 20. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 20% of 100

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 20% = 0.2
  2. Multiply: 100 × 0.2
  3. Result: 20

A 20% figure on 100 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $100 bill, 20% is $20, making the total $120. Split between two people that is $60 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% of 100?

20% of 100 is 20. Multiply 100 by 20 and divide by 100: 100 × 20 ÷ 100 = 20.

How do I calculate 20 percent of 100 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 100 is 10. Then scale: 20% is 2 times that, which gives 20.

What is 100 minus 20%?

100 − 20% = 80. Subtracting 20% is the same as multiplying by 0.8.

What is 100 plus 20%?

100 + 20% = 120. Adding 20% is the same as multiplying by 1.2.

20 is what percent of 100?

20 ÷ 100 × 100 = 20%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
