What is 40% of 300?

40% of 300 = 120

300 × 40 ÷ 100 = 120. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 40% of 300

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 40% = 0.4
  2. Multiply: 300 × 0.4
  3. Result: 120

As a discount, 40% off $300 saves $120 and leaves a sale price of $180. As a raise or increase, 40% on 300 takes it up to 420. The 120 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 40% of 300?

40% of 300 is 120. Multiply 300 by 40 and divide by 100: 300 × 40 ÷ 100 = 120.

How do I calculate 40 percent of 300 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 300 is 30. Then scale: 40% is 4 times that, which gives 120.

What is 300 minus 40%?

300 − 40% = 180. Subtracting 40% is the same as multiplying by 0.6.

What is 300 plus 40%?

300 + 40% = 420. Adding 40% is the same as multiplying by 1.4.

120 is what percent of 300?

120 ÷ 300 × 100 = 40%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
