What is 15% of 30?

15% of 30 = 4.5

30 × 15 ÷ 100 = 4.5. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 15% of 30

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 15% = 0.15
  2. Multiply: 30 × 0.15
  3. Result: 4.5

A 15% figure on 30 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $30 bill, 15% is $4.5, making the total $34.5. Split between two people that is $17.25 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 15% of 30?

15% of 30 is 4.5. Multiply 30 by 15 and divide by 100: 30 × 15 ÷ 100 = 4.5.

How do I calculate 15 percent of 30 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 30 is 3. Then scale: 15% is 1.5 times that, which gives 4.5.

What is 30 minus 15%?

30 − 15% = 25.5. Subtracting 15% is the same as multiplying by 0.85.

What is 30 plus 15%?

30 + 15% = 34.5. Adding 15% is the same as multiplying by 1.15.

4.5 is what percent of 30?

4.5 ÷ 30 × 100 = 15%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
