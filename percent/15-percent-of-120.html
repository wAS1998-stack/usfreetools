What is 15% of 120?

15% of 120 = 18

120 × 15 ÷ 100 = 18. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 15% of 120

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 15% = 0.15
  2. Multiply: 120 × 0.15
  3. Result: 18

A 15% figure on 120 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $120 bill, 15% is $18, making the total $138. Split between two people that is $69 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 15% of 120?

15% of 120 is 18. Multiply 120 by 15 and divide by 100: 120 × 15 ÷ 100 = 18.

How do I calculate 15 percent of 120 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 120 is 12. Then scale: 15% is 1.5 times that, which gives 18.

What is 120 minus 15%?

120 − 15% = 102. Subtracting 15% is the same as multiplying by 0.85.

What is 120 plus 15%?

120 + 15% = 138. Adding 15% is the same as multiplying by 1.15.

18 is what percent of 120?

18 ÷ 120 × 100 = 15%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
