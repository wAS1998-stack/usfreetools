What is 20% of 300?

20% of 300 = 60

300 × 20 ÷ 100 = 60. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 20% of 300

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 20% = 0.2
  2. Multiply: 300 × 0.2
  3. Result: 60

A 20% figure on 300 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $300 bill, 20% is $60, making the total $360. Split between two people that is $180 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% of 300?

20% of 300 is 60. Multiply 300 by 20 and divide by 100: 300 × 20 ÷ 100 = 60.

How do I calculate 20 percent of 300 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 300 is 30. Then scale: 20% is 2 times that, which gives 60.

What is 300 minus 20%?

300 − 20% = 240. Subtracting 20% is the same as multiplying by 0.8.

What is 300 plus 20%?

300 + 20% = 360. Adding 20% is the same as multiplying by 1.2.

60 is what percent of 300?

60 ÷ 300 × 100 = 20%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
