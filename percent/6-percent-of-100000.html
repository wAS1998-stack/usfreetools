What is 6% of 100,000?

6% of 100,000 = 6,000

100,000 × 6 ÷ 100 = 6,000. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 6% of 100,000

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 6% = 0.06
  2. Multiply: 100,000 × 0.06
  3. Result: 6,000
