What is 7.5% of 240?

7.5% of 240 = 18

240 × 7.5 ÷ 100 = 18. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 7.5% of 240

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 7.5% = 0.075
  2. Multiply: 240 × 0.075
  3. Result: 18

7.5% is a typical sales-tax or VAT rate. Tax on $240 at 7.5% comes to $18, so the price with tax is $258. If instead $240 already includes 7.5% tax, the pre-tax price was $223.26.
