What is 80% of 30?

80% of 30 = 24

30 × 80 ÷ 100 = 24. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 80% of 30

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 80% = 0.8
  2. Multiply: 30 × 0.8
  3. Result: 24

As a discount, 80% off $30 saves $24 and leaves a sale price of $6. As a raise or increase, 80% on 30 takes it up to 54. The 24 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 80% of 30?

80% of 30 is 24. Multiply 30 by 80 and divide by 100: 30 × 80 ÷ 100 = 24.

How do I calculate 80 percent of 30 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 30 is 3. Then scale: 80% is 8 times that, which gives 24.

What is 30 minus 80%?

30 − 80% = 6. Subtracting 80% is the same as multiplying by 0.2.

What is 30 plus 80%?

30 + 80% = 54. Adding 80% is the same as multiplying by 1.8.

24 is what percent of 30?

24 ÷ 30 × 100 = 80%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
