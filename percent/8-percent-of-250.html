What is 8% of 250?

8% of 250 = 20

250 × 8 ÷ 100 = 20. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 8% of 250

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 8% = 0.08
  2. Multiply: 250 × 0.08
  3. Result: 20

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 8% of 250?

How do I calculate 8 percent of 250 in my head?

Find 1% first: 1% of 250 is 2.5. Then multiply by 8: 20.

What is 250 minus 8%?

250 − 8% = 230. Subtracting 8% is the same as multiplying by 0.92.

What is 250 plus 8%?

250 + 8% = 270. Adding 8% is the same as multiplying by 1.08.

20 is what percent of 250?
