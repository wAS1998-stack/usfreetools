What is 7.5% of 1,300?

7.5% of 1,300 = 97.5

1,300 × 7.5 ÷ 100 = 97.5. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 7.5% of 1,300

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 7.5% = 0.075
  2. Multiply: 1,300 × 0.075
  3. Result: 97.5
