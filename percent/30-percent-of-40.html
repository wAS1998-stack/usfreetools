What is 30% of 40?

30% of 40 = 12

40 × 30 ÷ 100 = 12. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 30% of 40

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 30% = 0.3
  2. Multiply: 40 × 0.3
  3. Result: 12

As a discount, 30% off $40 saves $12 and leaves a sale price of $28. As a raise or increase, 30% on 40 takes it up to 52. The 12 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 30% of 40?

30% of 40 is 12. Multiply 40 by 30 and divide by 100: 40 × 30 ÷ 100 = 12.

How do I calculate 30 percent of 40 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 40 is 4. Then scale: 30% is 3 times that, which gives 12.

What is 40 minus 30%?

40 − 30% = 28. Subtracting 30% is the same as multiplying by 0.7.

What is 40 plus 30%?

40 + 30% = 52. Adding 30% is the same as multiplying by 1.3.

12 is what percent of 40?

12 ÷ 40 × 100 = 30%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
