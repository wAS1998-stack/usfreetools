What is 2% of 30?

2% of 30 = 0.6

30 × 2 ÷ 100 = 0.6. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 2% of 30

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 2% = 0.02
  2. Multiply: 30 × 0.02
  3. Result: 0.6

Small percentages like 2% usually show up as interest, fees, inflation or commission. 2% of 30 is 0.6 — on a 30 balance at 2% a year, that is the annual interest, or about 0.05 per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 2% of 30?

2% of 30 is 0.6. Multiply 30 by 2 and divide by 100: 30 × 2 ÷ 100 = 0.6.

How do I calculate 2 percent of 30 in my head?

Find 1% first: 1% of 30 is 0.3. Then multiply by 2: 0.6.

What is 30 minus 2%?

30 − 2% = 29.4. Subtracting 2% is the same as multiplying by 0.98.

What is 30 plus 2%?

30 + 2% = 30.6. Adding 2% is the same as multiplying by 1.02.

0.6 is what percent of 30?

0.6 ÷ 30 × 100 = 2%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
