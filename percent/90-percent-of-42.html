What is 90% of 42?

90% of 42 = 37.8

42 × 90 ÷ 100 = 37.8. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 90% of 42

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 90% = 0.9
  2. Multiply: 42 × 0.9
  3. Result: 37.8

90% of 42 is 37.8. Put another way, 37.8 is 90% of 42, and 42 is 111.11% of 37.8 — percentages are just fractions of 100, so any of the three numbers can be found from the other two.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 90% of 42?

90% of 42 is 37.8. Multiply 42 by 90 and divide by 100: 42 × 90 ÷ 100 = 37.8.

How do I calculate 90 percent of 42 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 42 is 4.2. Then scale: 90% is 9 times that, which gives 37.8.

What is 42 minus 90%?

42 − 90% = 4.2. Subtracting 90% is the same as multiplying by 0.1.

What is 42 plus 90%?

42 + 90% = 79.8. Adding 90% is the same as multiplying by 1.9.

37.8 is what percent of 42?

37.8 ÷ 42 × 100 = 90%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
