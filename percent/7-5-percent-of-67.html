What is 7.5% of 67?

67 × 7.5 ÷ 100 = 5.03. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 7.5% of 67

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 7.5% = 0.075
  2. Multiply: 67 × 0.075
  3. Result: 5.03
