What is 10% of 550?

10% of 550 = 55

550 × 10 ÷ 100 = 55. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 10% of 550

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 10% = 0.1
  2. Multiply: 550 × 0.1
  3. Result: 55

10% is a typical sales-tax or VAT rate. Tax on $550 at 10% comes to $55, so the price with tax is $605. If instead $550 already includes 10% tax, the pre-tax price was $500.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 10% of 550?

10% of 550 is 55. Multiply 550 by 10 and divide by 100: 550 × 10 ÷ 100 = 55.

How do I calculate 10 percent of 550 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 550 is 55. Then scale: 10% is 1 times that, which gives 55.

What is 550 minus 10%?

550 − 10% = 495. Subtracting 10% is the same as multiplying by 0.9.

What is 550 plus 10%?

550 + 10% = 605. Adding 10% is the same as multiplying by 1.1.

55 is what percent of 550?

55 ÷ 550 × 100 = 10%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
