What is 20% of 120?

20% of 120 = 24

120 × 20 ÷ 100 = 24. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 20% of 120

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 20% = 0.2
  2. Multiply: 120 × 0.2
  3. Result: 24

A 20% figure on 120 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $120 bill, 20% is $24, making the total $144. Split between two people that is $72 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% of 120?

20% of 120 is 24. Multiply 120 by 20 and divide by 100: 120 × 20 ÷ 100 = 24.

How do I calculate 20 percent of 120 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 120 is 12. Then scale: 20% is 2 times that, which gives 24.

What is 120 minus 20%?

120 − 20% = 96. Subtracting 20% is the same as multiplying by 0.8.

What is 120 plus 20%?

120 + 20% = 144. Adding 20% is the same as multiplying by 1.2.

24 is what percent of 120?

24 ÷ 120 × 100 = 20%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
