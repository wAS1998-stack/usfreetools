What is 80% of 300?

80% of 300 = 240

300 × 80 ÷ 100 = 240. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 80% of 300

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 80% = 0.8
  2. Multiply: 300 × 0.8
  3. Result: 240

As a discount, 80% off $300 saves $240 and leaves a sale price of $60. As a raise or increase, 80% on 300 takes it up to 540. The 240 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 80% of 300?

80% of 300 is 240. Multiply 300 by 80 and divide by 100: 300 × 80 ÷ 100 = 240.

How do I calculate 80 percent of 300 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 300 is 30. Then scale: 80% is 8 times that, which gives 240.

What is 300 minus 80%?

300 − 80% = 60. Subtracting 80% is the same as multiplying by 0.2.

What is 300 plus 80%?

300 + 80% = 540. Adding 80% is the same as multiplying by 1.8.

240 is what percent of 300?

240 ÷ 300 × 100 = 80%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
