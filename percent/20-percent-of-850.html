What is 20% of 850?

20% of 850 = 170

850 × 20 ÷ 100 = 170. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 20% of 850

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 20% = 0.2
  2. Multiply: 850 × 0.2
  3. Result: 170

As a discount, 20% off $850 saves $170 and leaves a sale price of $680. As a raise or increase, 20% on 850 takes it up to 1,020. The 170 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% of 850?

20% of 850 is 170. Multiply 850 by 20 and divide by 100: 850 × 20 ÷ 100 = 170.

How do I calculate 20 percent of 850 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 850 is 85. Then scale: 20% is 2 times that, which gives 170.

What is 850 minus 20%?

850 − 20% = 680. Subtracting 20% is the same as multiplying by 0.8.

What is 850 plus 20%?

850 + 20% = 1,020. Adding 20% is the same as multiplying by 1.2.

170 is what percent of 850?

170 ÷ 850 × 100 = 20%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
