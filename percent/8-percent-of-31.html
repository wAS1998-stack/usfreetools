What is 8% of 31?

8% of 31 = 2.48

31 × 8 ÷ 100 = 2.48. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 8% of 31

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 8% = 0.08
  2. Multiply: 31 × 0.08
  3. Result: 2.48

8% is a typical sales-tax or VAT rate. Tax on $31 at 8% comes to $2.48, so the price with tax is $33.48. If instead $31 already includes 8% tax, the pre-tax price was $28.7.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 8% of 31?

8% of 31 is 2.48. Multiply 31 by 8 and divide by 100: 31 × 8 ÷ 100 = 2.48.

How do I calculate 8 percent of 31 in my head?

Find 1% first: 1% of 31 is 0.31. Then multiply by 8: 2.48.

What is 31 minus 8%?

31 − 8% = 28.52. Subtracting 8% is the same as multiplying by 0.92.

What is 31 plus 8%?

31 + 8% = 33.48. Adding 8% is the same as multiplying by 1.08.

2.48 is what percent of 31?

2.48 ÷ 31 × 100 = 8%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
