What is 20% of 15?

20% of 15 = 3

15 × 20 ÷ 100 = 3. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 20% of 15

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 20% = 0.2
  2. Multiply: 15 × 0.2
  3. Result: 3

A 20% figure on 15 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $15 bill, 20% is $3, making the total $18. Split between two people that is $9 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% of 15?

20% of 15 is 3. Multiply 15 by 20 and divide by 100: 15 × 20 ÷ 100 = 3.

How do I calculate 20 percent of 15 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 15 is 1.5. Then scale: 20% is 2 times that, which gives 3.

What is 15 minus 20%?

15 − 20% = 12. Subtracting 20% is the same as multiplying by 0.8.

What is 15 plus 20%?

15 + 20% = 18. Adding 20% is the same as multiplying by 1.2.

3 is what percent of 15?

3 ÷ 15 × 100 = 20%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
