What is 20% of 3?

20% of 3 = 0.6

3 × 20 ÷ 100 = 0.6. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 20% of 3

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 20% = 0.2
  2. Multiply: 3 × 0.2
  3. Result: 0.6

A 20% figure on 3 is most often a restaurant tip: on a $3 bill, 20% is $0.6, making the total $3.6. Split between two people that is $1.8 each.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 20% of 3?

20% of 3 is 0.6. Multiply 3 by 20 and divide by 100: 3 × 20 ÷ 100 = 0.6.

How do I calculate 20 percent of 3 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 3 is 0.3. Then scale: 20% is 2 times that, which gives 0.6.

What is 3 minus 20%?

3 − 20% = 2.4. Subtracting 20% is the same as multiplying by 0.8.

What is 3 plus 20%?

3 + 20% = 3.6. Adding 20% is the same as multiplying by 1.2.

0.6 is what percent of 3?

0.6 ÷ 3 × 100 = 20%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
