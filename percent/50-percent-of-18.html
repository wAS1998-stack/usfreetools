What is 50% of 18?

50% of 18 = 9

18 × 50 ÷ 100 = 9. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 50% of 18

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 50% = 0.5
  2. Multiply: 18 × 0.5
  3. Result: 9

As a discount, 50% off $18 saves $9 and leaves a sale price of $9. As a raise or increase, 50% on 18 takes it up to 27. The 9 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 50% of 18?

50% of 18 is 9. Multiply 18 by 50 and divide by 100: 18 × 50 ÷ 100 = 9.

How do I calculate 50 percent of 18 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 18 is 1.8. Then scale: 50% is 5 times that, which gives 9.

What is 18 minus 50%?

18 − 50% = 9. Subtracting 50% is the same as multiplying by 0.5.

What is 18 plus 50%?

18 + 50% = 27. Adding 50% is the same as multiplying by 1.5.

9 is what percent of 18?

9 ÷ 18 × 100 = 50%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
