What is 50% of 80?

50% of 80 = 40

80 × 50 ÷ 100 = 40. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 50% of 80

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 50% = 0.5
  2. Multiply: 80 × 0.5
  3. Result: 40

As a discount, 50% off $80 saves $40 and leaves a sale price of $40. As a raise or increase, 50% on 80 takes it up to 120. The 40 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 50% of 80?

50% of 80 is 40. Multiply 80 by 50 and divide by 100: 80 × 50 ÷ 100 = 40.

How do I calculate 50 percent of 80 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 80 is 8. Then scale: 50% is 5 times that, which gives 40.

What is 80 minus 50%?

80 − 50% = 40. Subtracting 50% is the same as multiplying by 0.5.

What is 80 plus 50%?

80 + 50% = 120. Adding 50% is the same as multiplying by 1.5.

40 is what percent of 80?

40 ÷ 80 × 100 = 50%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
