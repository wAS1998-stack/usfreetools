What is 10% of 10?

10% of 10 = 1

10 × 10 ÷ 100 = 1. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 10% of 10

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 10% = 0.1
  2. Multiply: 10 × 0.1
  3. Result: 1

As a discount, 10% off $10 saves $1 and leaves a sale price of $9. As a raise or increase, 10% on 10 takes it up to 11. The 1 figure is the same either way; only the direction changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 10% of 10?

10% of 10 is 1. Multiply 10 by 10 and divide by 100: 10 × 10 ÷ 100 = 1.

How do I calculate 10 percent of 10 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 10 is 1. Then scale: 10% is 1 times that, which gives 1.

What is 10 minus 10%?

10 − 10% = 9. Subtracting 10% is the same as multiplying by 0.9.

What is 10 plus 10%?

10 + 10% = 11. Adding 10% is the same as multiplying by 1.1.

1 is what percent of 10?

1 ÷ 10 × 100 = 10%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
