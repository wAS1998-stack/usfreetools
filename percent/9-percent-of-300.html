What is 9% of 300?

9% of 300 = 27

300 × 9 ÷ 100 = 27. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 9% of 300

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 9% = 0.09
  2. Multiply: 300 × 0.09
  3. Result: 27

9% is a typical sales-tax or VAT rate. Tax on $300 at 9% comes to $27, so the price with tax is $327. If instead $300 already includes 9% tax, the pre-tax price was $275.23.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 9% of 300?

9% of 300 is 27. Multiply 300 by 9 and divide by 100: 300 × 9 ÷ 100 = 27.

How do I calculate 9 percent of 300 in my head?

Find 1% first: 1% of 300 is 3. Then multiply by 9: 27.

What is 300 minus 9%?

300 − 9% = 273. Subtracting 9% is the same as multiplying by 0.91.

What is 300 plus 9%?

300 + 9% = 327. Adding 9% is the same as multiplying by 1.09.

27 is what percent of 300?

27 ÷ 300 × 100 = 9%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
