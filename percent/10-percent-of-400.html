What is 10% of 400?

10% of 400 = 40

400 × 10 ÷ 100 = 40. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 10% of 400

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 10% = 0.1
  2. Multiply: 400 × 0.1
  3. Result: 40

10% is a typical sales-tax or VAT rate. Tax on $400 at 10% comes to $40, so the price with tax is $440. If instead $400 already includes 10% tax, the pre-tax price was $363.64.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 10% of 400?

10% of 400 is 40. Multiply 400 by 10 and divide by 100: 400 × 10 ÷ 100 = 40.

How do I calculate 10 percent of 400 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 400 is 40. Then scale: 10% is 1 times that, which gives 40.

What is 400 minus 10%?

400 − 10% = 360. Subtracting 10% is the same as multiplying by 0.9.

What is 400 plus 10%?

400 + 10% = 440. Adding 10% is the same as multiplying by 1.1.

40 is what percent of 400?

40 ÷ 400 × 100 = 10%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
