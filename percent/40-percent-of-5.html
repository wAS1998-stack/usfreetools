What is 40% of 5?

40% of 5 = 2

5 × 40 ÷ 100 = 2. Change either number below for any other percentage.

How to work out 40% of 5

  1. Write the percentage as a decimal: 40% = 0.4
  2. Multiply: 5 × 0.4
  3. Result: 2

40% of 5 is 2. Put another way, 2 is 40% of 5, and 5 is 250% of 2 — percentages are just fractions of 100, so any of the three numbers can be found from the other two.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 40% of 5?

40% of 5 is 2. Multiply 5 by 40 and divide by 100: 5 × 40 ÷ 100 = 2.

How do I calculate 40 percent of 5 in my head?

Find 10% first by moving the decimal point: 10% of 5 is 0.5. Then scale: 40% is 4 times that, which gives 2.

What is 5 minus 40%?

5 − 40% = 3. Subtracting 40% is the same as multiplying by 0.6.

What is 5 plus 40%?

5 + 40% = 7. Adding 40% is the same as multiplying by 1.4.

2 is what percent of 5?

2 ÷ 5 × 100 = 40%. That is the reverse of this page's calculation.
