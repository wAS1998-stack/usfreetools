Typical figures — moisture, particle size and compaction change them, so check with your supplier before booking a truck or trailer. Many half-ton pickups can’t safely carry a full cubic yard of gravel or wet soil — check your payload rating first.

How to calculate cubic yards

Volume is area × depth, and a cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so the whole calculation is: length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (ft) ÷ 27. The only trap is depth, which is almost always measured in inches. Divide inches by 12 first: 4 inches is 0.333 ft, 3 inches is 0.25 ft.

A worked example: a 12 × 15 ft garden bed filled 6 inches deep is 12 × 15 × 0.5 = 90 cubic feet, and 90 ÷ 27 = 3.33 cubic yards. Round up when you order — suppliers usually sell in quarter or half yards — and add 5–10% for settling, uneven ground and spillage.

For a circle — a round flower bed, a tree ring, a post hole or a column form — the area is π × radius², or 0.785 × diameter². A bed 10 ft across filled 4 inches deep is 78.5 sq ft × 0.333 ft = 26.2 cubic feet, just under one cubic yard. For a triangle, the area is half of base × height. Irregular spaces split into rectangles, circles and triangles; add the pieces together.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate cubic yards?

Multiply length × width × depth with all three in feet, then divide by 27. Convert inches to feet first by dividing by 12. A 10 × 10 ft area 4 inches deep is 10 × 10 × 0.333 = 33.3 cubic feet, or 1.23 cubic yards.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

27. A cubic yard is a cube 3 feet on each side, and 3 × 3 × 3 = 27 cubic feet. In metric it is 0.765 cubic meters.

How many square feet does a cubic yard cover?

Divide 324 by the depth in inches. At 2 inches a cubic yard covers 162 square feet, at 3 inches 108, at 4 inches 81 and at 6 inches 54.

How do I calculate cubic yards for a circle?

Find the area with 0.785 × diameter², multiply by the depth in feet, and divide by 27. A round bed 10 feet across, 4 inches deep: 78.5 × 0.333 ÷ 27 = 0.97 cubic yards.
