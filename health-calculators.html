What these numbers can and can't tell you

  • BMI is a quick screening number based on height and weight. It does not separate muscle from fat, so athletes and older adults can be misclassified.
  • Calorie targets use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the formula most dietitians start from. Treat the result as a starting point and adjust after a few weeks of real-world results.
  • Heat index and wind chill use the US National Weather Service formulas, so the numbers match the ones in weather forecasts.
